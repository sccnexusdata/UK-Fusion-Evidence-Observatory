Research boundaries

Scope of scientific evidence

The SCC Nexus Fusion project distinguishes primary experiment records, source publications and validated findings. No unreviewed discovery candidate is automatically public.

FAIR-MAST shot records establish metadata and traceability, not successful physics outcomes.
UKAEA publications identify research claims. Their existence is not independent validation of every result.
Crossref, DataCite and OpenAlex are discovery and identity aids; metadata agreement is not scientific corroboration.
Simulations, diagnostic observations, performance claims and commercial electricity production require distinct evidence.

Method

A reproducible chain of custody

The private Engine maintains retrieval metadata, source identifiers and a human review ledger. The public repository independently validates approved, allow-listed releases.

01 Discover — bounded primary-source acquisition with provenance and source-health checks.
02 Corroborate — exact identifier matching and appropriately independent supporting sources.
03 Review — explicitly recorded human decision, with limitations and claim boundaries.
04 Publish — independently validated public JSON bundle, SHA-256 checksums and a dated release manifest.

Academic submissions

Materials available to scrutinise

Independent readers should inspect the published record, machine-readable sources, stated limitations, release manifest and validator, and should request clarification where a claim exceeds its source.

Public methodology and limitations pages explain the evidence gate.
Validated public JSON and integrity manifest are accessible from the published evidence register.
Open source repository provides independent validation scripts and corrections route.
Restricted reviewer notes and unfinished acquisition candidates are not peer-reviewed evidence.
