Non-negotiable rules

Designed to resist confident nonsense.

01

Discovery ≠ evidence

Finding a record makes it a candidate, not a conclusion.

02

Identity ≠ claim truth

Correctly matching an organisation or paper does not validate what it says.

03

ML output ≠ evidence

Automation can assist review but cannot manufacture scientific verification.

04

Centrality ≠ importance

Network prominence is descriptive, never a substitute for scientific significance.
