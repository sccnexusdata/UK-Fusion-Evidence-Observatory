THE PROGRAMME

A clearer view of fusion's progress.

Fusion is technically demanding, publicly significant and often described in ambitious terms. SCC Nexus examines the underlying evidence without treating a press release, simulation or programme target as an achieved scientific result.

The purpose is to make UK fusion evidence easier for researchers, decision-makers, industry and interested members of the public to inspect and challenge.
