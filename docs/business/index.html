Decision support

Measure delivery, not just ambition

Business and funding reviews must distinguish programme commitments, contracts and achievements. The current evidence release is too limited to underpin investment or commercial-readiness conclusions.

Publication of a strategy is evidence of an announced policy, not technical delivery.
An announced allocation is not automatically a payment, contract or completed project.
An identified business or supply-chain entity is not proof of commercial readiness.
A proposed action or future milestone is not evidence it has been achieved.

Due diligence

Commercial questions worth testing

These are evidence-led research questions, not unverified conclusions or forecasts.

What is the primary source and date of each stated UK fusion commitment?
Which grants, tenders, awards or payments are independently identifiable?
Which industrial participants are verified through stable company or contract identifiers?
What regulatory, engineering and project-delivery events are evidenced as completed?

Evidence controls

Risk and independence safeguards

The publication firewall applies equally to business, government and scientific claims. Funding, sponsorship and editorial review should be disclosed separately from evidence verification.

Distinguish independent analysis from official affiliation or endorsement.
Do not infer investment value from patent counts, network centrality or ML output.
Describe uncertainties, evidence age, source conflicts and missing coverage.
Keep commercially sensitive material and unpublished reviewer records off public pages.

Submission framework

External academic and business review

A submission-ready dossier should state its purpose, research methods, provenance, conclusions, declared limitations, commercial interests and version number.

Executive abstract and decision question anchored to evidence records.
Methods and replicable sources with citations and retrieval timestamps.
Review register including counter-evidence, limitations and corrections.
No claim of peer review, UKAEA partnership or validated performance without proof.
