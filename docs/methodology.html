Transparent method

Methodology

Every public record has a stable identifier, neutral summary, evidence classification, named publisher, source URL, retrieval date, verification status, confidence class and explicit limitation.

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Legislation, official datasets and primary programme documents.
  2. Peer-reviewed research and recognised scientific repositories.
  3. Contracts, company filings, patents and original organisational publications.
  4. Attributed secondary reporting where primary evidence is unavailable.

Publication controls

Collection and internal review take place in a private repository. Only approved fields and records can enter a signed-off bundle. This public repository independently checks its schema, source register, record count and SHA-256 manifest before deployment.

Interpretation

A high confidence class means the stated record is well supported by its source. It does not mean that a forecast will happen. Programme commitments, experimental measurements and commercial outcomes remain separate evidence types.
