Transparent method

How evidence earns its place.

Every public record has a stable identifier, neutral summary, evidence classification, named publisher, source URL, retrieval date, verification status, confidence class and explicit limitation.

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Legislation, official datasets and primary programme documents.
  2. Peer-reviewed research and recognised scientific repositories.
  3. Contracts, company filings, patents and original organisational publications.
  4. Attributed secondary reporting where primary evidence is unavailable.

Publication controls

Collection and internal review take place in a private repository. Only approved fields and records can enter a signed-off bundle. This public repository independently checks its schema, source register, record count and SHA-256 manifest before deployment.

The review pipeline fails closed: automated or assisted annotation cannot itself establish a scientific claim or publication approval. A recorded human decision is required before those states can be represented as verified.

Prospective performance gate

The architectural firewall is a safety control, not evidence that the review system performs well. The Observatory therefore does not claim reviewer accuracy, diagnostic interpretation accuracy or scientific-validation performance merely because the pipeline rejects unsafe states.

Before any quantitative performance claim is published, a bounded pilot protocol must be frozen in advance. That protocol must state the number and selection logic for test cases, case-mix and difficulty criteria, human-reference process, agreement metric, provenance-completeness metric, allowable unexplained-conflict rate and explicit stop/review thresholds. Thresholds must be committed before outcomes are inspected and may not be relaxed afterwards to manufacture a pass.

Current status: no public quantitative performance gate has yet been completed. Until one is prospectively defined and run, Fusion Evidence should be described as architecturally controlled and provenance-led, not as a validated high-accuracy scientific classifier.

Interpretation

A high confidence class means the stated record is well supported by its source. It does not mean that a forecast will happen. Programme commitments, experimental measurements and commercial outcomes remain separate evidence types.
