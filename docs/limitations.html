Limitations

  • The initial release demonstrates the repository and publication controls; it is not a complete sector census.
  • Official publication proves that a statement was made, not that a target will be achieved.
  • Commercial organisations disclose different levels of technical and financial detail.
  • Open bibliographic, company and procurement data contain omissions and matching errors.
  • Scientific gain, engineering gain and net electricity are not interchangeable.
  • Environmental impacts depend on plant design, materials, location and operating assumptions.

The observatory does not provide investment advice, regulatory approval, engineering certification or a forecast of commercial fusion dates.
