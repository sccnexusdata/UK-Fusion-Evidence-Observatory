Read before interpretation

What this observatory cannot prove.

A credible evidence system is defined as much by its boundaries as by its contents. These limitations are part of the publication, not fine print.

Current scope

  • The initial release demonstrates repository and publication controls; it is not a complete sector census.
  • Official publication proves that a statement was made, not that a target will be achieved.
  • Commercial organisations disclose different levels of technical and financial detail.
  • Open bibliographic, company and procurement data contain omissions and matching errors.
  • Scientific gain, engineering gain and net electricity are not interchangeable.
  • Environmental impacts depend on plant design, materials, location and operating assumptions.

No implied endorsement

Inclusion of an organisation, programme, paper, company or source does not imply endorsement. Network prominence, publication volume and source frequency are descriptive signals only and are not treated as measures of scientific importance.

No performance claim by architecture

A fail-closed workflow reduces the risk of unsafe publication states. It does not establish that reviewers, classifiers or diagnostic interpretation are scientifically accurate. Quantitative performance claims require a prospective, frozen validation protocol.

No advisory role

The Observatory does not provide investment advice, regulatory approval, engineering certification or a forecast of commercial fusion dates.
